# Asa (آسا) — Website and Brand Brief

## Brand idea

**Brand:** آسا / Asa  
**Vision:** Family support, companionship, and peace of mind.  
**Launch promise:** Defined, local help in one Tehran service area, delivered with transparency and dignity.

### Positioning options

**Recommended English:** Asa helps you take care of family in Tehran when you cannot be there in person.  
**Recommended Persian:** آسا؛ کمک مطمئن برای مراقبت از خانواده، وقتی خودتان نمی‌توانید کنارشان باشید.

Alternative Persian direction:  
**آسا؛ خیال راحتِ فرزندان، زندگی بهترِ سالمندان** — warmer, but use only if the service proof supports the promise.

## Service naming architecture

- **آسا همراه / Asa Hamrah:** practical companionship for family tasks, errands, and travel/transport support.
- **آسا خوراک / Asa Khoraak:** packaged food, varied meals, and daily or bulk meal plans.
- **آسا پزشک / Asa Pezeshk:** appointment booking, medical follow-up, and practical non-clinical accompaniment to treatment centers.

## Core user journey

1. Visitor understands the service in one sentence.
2. Chooses Asa Hamrah, Asa Khoraak, or Asa Pezeshk.
3. Enters recipient neighborhood and checks service area.
4. Describes list/appointment and timing.
5. Sees scope, pass-through costs, fees, and cancellation rules.
6. Completes consent and authorization.
7. Pays only through the approved method.
8. Receives confirmation and service updates.
9. Receives completion evidence/family update.
10. Reorders or requests another service.

## FAQ

**Can I order from outside Iran?**  
Only when Asa has a verified, approved payment route for your country and the exact service model. Payment availability must be disclosed accurately.

**What are Asa Hamrah, Asa Khoraak, and Asa Pezeshk?**  
Asa Hamrah is practical companionship and task support. Asa Khoraak is packaged food and meal-plan delivery. Asa Pezeshk coordinates medical-family logistics and non-clinical accompaniment; it does not replace a healthcare professional.

**Is the food or grocery cost included?**  
Product and meal value is shown separately from Asa’s service and delivery fees. The quote shows each component.

**Can Asa give medical advice?**  
No. Asa provides practical, non-clinical coordination and accompaniment. Asa does not diagnose, treat, interpret results, administer medication, or replace a healthcare professional.

**Will my family member receive a report?**  
Only with appropriate recipient/customer consent and only with factual information within the agreed scope.

**What if an item is unavailable?**  
The order uses the customer’s substitution rules and budget. Unapproved substitutions are not made silently.

**What if plans change?**  
Cancellation and rescheduling rules are shown before confirmation. Any additional travel or waiting cost must be stated in advance where possible.

**Do you operate throughout Iran?**  
No. Asa begins with a defined Tehran service area and expands only when staffing, travel times, quality, and permissions support it.

## Family Update example

- Visit completed: 10:00–13:00.
- Grocery shopping: completed.
- Delivery: confirmed with recipient.
- Appointment: attended and registration support provided.
- Follow-up: requested by the recipient / not requested.
- General observations: factual and within the companion’s role.
- Action for family: identified if consented.

Never call this a medical assessment.

## Minimum launch functionality

- Mobile-first bilingual pages.
- Short service request forms.
- Service-area checker.
- Human quote and confirmation workflow.
- Consent capture.
- Secure storage with limited access.
- Support phone/WhatsApp/email only after privacy review.
- Order status and receipt upload.
- Basic analytics with payer country and recipient city separated.

Optional later: customer portal, recurring scheduling, provider integrations, automated routing, and mobile app.

## Accessibility and trust requirements

Use readable Persian typography, strong contrast, keyboard-accessible forms, large tap targets, plain language, visible support contact, no hidden fees, no false urgency, and no unsupported safety or health claims.
