# Asa (آسا) — Launch Decision Memo

## Decision

**Do not launch the paid international-payment model as currently described. Proceed with validation and redesign only.**

## Why

The service problem is credible, and Tehran is a reasonable conditional pilot location. But the payment route is not proven. The exact transaction includes a foreign payer, an Iran-based recipient, Iran-based fulfillment, local suppliers/workers, and potentially health-related logistics. Provider contracts and sanctions/AML controls can reject the flow even if a country's law does not impose a blanket ban.

## Permitted next move

Conduct a non-transactional validation program: interviews, service-area mapping, worker recruitment, supplier quotes, mock order forms, provider outreach, legal review, and pricing tests that do not imply payment availability.

If a fully disclosed payment and settlement route is approved in writing, run a 5–10 order pilot in one Tehran zone. Keep the U.S. market excluded unless separately cleared.

## Ranked decisions

1. **Payment route:** obtain written approval from the exact provider and settlement bank.
2. **Entity and compliance:** complete sanctions, payment-perimeter, tax, privacy, and Iranian operations review.
3. **Tehran zone:** choose a 2–4 neighborhood pilot based on travel time and worker coverage.
4. **Wedge:** test Shopping and Medical Companion as separate offers.
5. **Scale gate:** require positive contribution margin, on-time completion, no severe incidents, and repeat/referral evidence before expansion.

## Red lines

No concealed beneficiaries, false invoices, third-party accounts, transaction splitting, disguised service descriptions, unsupported crypto workaround, clinical care, medication handling, or accepting paid orders before the gates pass.
