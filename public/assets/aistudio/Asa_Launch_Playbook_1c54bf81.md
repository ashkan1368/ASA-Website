# Asa (آسا) — Launch Playbook

## Decision rule

Do not accept paid orders until the payment, legal, insurance, safeguarding, privacy, and operating gates pass. The plan below starts with validation and only becomes a launch plan if the gates are cleared.

## First 30 days — validate the opportunity

| Days | Workstream | Owner | Evidence of completion | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Freeze service scope and exclusions | Founder | One-page service spec in Persian/English | No clinical or unapproved activity |
| 1–5 | Entity, ownership, U.S./EU/UK/CA/AU/TR nexus memo | Counsel/accountant | Signed memo and open questions | Required before provider outreach |
| 1–7 | Map payer → provider → settlement bank → Iranian suppliers/workers | Founder + compliance | End-to-end diagram | No unknown economic beneficiary |
| 1–10 | Obtain written provider and bank responses | Founder | Exact use-case approval or rejection | Payment gate |
| 1–10 | Screen proposed owners, workers, vendors, and counterparties | Compliance owner | Screening log and escalation path | No unresolved credible matches |
| 4–12 | Interview 10 diaspora payers, 5 recipients, 5 local workers, 5 suppliers/partners | Founder | Notes, consent, coded themes | No fabricated demand claims |
| 5–12 | Compare 2–4 Tehran neighborhoods | Ops lead | Travel-time, supplier, worker, clinic map | Pick one zone |
| 8–14 | Recruit and vet 3–5 workers | Ops lead | IDs, references, training checklist | No solo work before safeguards |
| 10–16 | Quote local travel, labor, insurance, legal, and payment costs | Finance lead | Quote register | Model updated |
| 12–18 | Build bilingual landing page and request form | Founder/contractor | Working form and test orders | No payment promise until cleared |
| 15–22 | Run smoke tests with request forms and manual quotes | Founder | 20 qualified inquiries or clear failure evidence | Measure real behavior |
| 20–30 | If all gates pass, run 5–10 paid pilot orders | Ops lead | Completed jobs, receipts, reports, refunds | Paid pilot gate |

### Interview questions

Ask about the last time the person needed help, what they did, what failed, who paid, how long it took, what evidence they wanted, and what they actually paid. Ask for a real recent example before asking what they think of Asa. Do not ask leading questions such as “Would you love this service?”

### Minimum pilot evidence

Proceed to a conditional launch only if:

- At least 5 customers complete paid orders.
- Contribution margin is positive after direct labor, travel, payment costs, and failure reserve.
- At least 80% of services complete within the promised window.
- All family updates are sent when consented.
- No severe safeguarding, privacy, or payment incident occurs.
- At least 20% of pilot customers either reorder or make a qualified referral within the review period.

These are provisional internal thresholds, not industry benchmarks.

## Following 90 days — conditional weekly roadmap

### Weeks 1–2: controlled launch

- Open only the approved Tehran zone.
- Offer Shopping and Medical Companion as separate products.
- Human-confirm every order.
- Review every exception daily.

### Weeks 3–4: quality stabilization

- Audit 100% of receipts and family updates.
- Measure travel time, worker utilization, substitutions, refunds, and complaint reasons.
- Remove any service step that creates unclear liability.

### Weeks 5–6: acquisition tests

- Run one search campaign and one community/referral campaign in a cleared market.
- Use unique source codes.
- Keep spend capped until paid conversion and contribution margin are known.

### Weeks 7–8: repeat behavior

- Offer a prepaid 3-order shopping bundle to customers with repeated need.
- Test a monthly reminder, not auto-renewal.
- Ask for consented testimonials only after service completion.

### Weeks 9–10: partner test

- Approach authorized clinics, labs, suppliers, or community organizations with a factual one-page partner brief.
- Do not pay or accept undisclosed clinical referrals.
- Require clear role boundaries.

### Weeks 11–12: expansion decision

Expand only if the zone is operationally stable, payment route remains approved, contribution margin is positive, and staffing capacity is not overloaded. Otherwise narrow the service, raise the minimum fee, change the zone, or stop.

## Founder vs delegation

**Founder:** entity/payment decisions, provider correspondence, first 10 customer calls, pricing tests, partner negotiations, incident escalation, weekly financial review.  
**Delegate after training:** shopping, delivery, appointment accompaniment, receipt capture, routine customer updates.  
**Do not delegate without specialist review:** sanctions decisions, health-data policy, insurance interpretation, clinical scope, or payment-perimeter classification.

## Stop conditions

Stop paid launch if: provider approval is verbal only; the bank cannot confirm the route; a customer requests concealment; a worker cannot be screened; service scope enters medicine/clinical care; insurance is unavailable; contribution margin is negative; or complaint/refund rates show a repeated quality failure.
