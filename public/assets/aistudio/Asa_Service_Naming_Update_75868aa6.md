# Asa (آسا) — Official Service Naming Update

## Founder-approved service architecture

### آسا همراه — Asa Hamrah

**Meaning:** accompanying family members for practical tasks, errands, and travel/transport support.

Potential services include accompanying a parent to a treatment center, helping with registration and waiting-room logistics, shopping and errands, and other defined practical tasks. The exact launch scope must remain non-clinical unless a separately licensed and insured service is later approved.

### آسا خوراک — Asa Khoraak

**Meaning:** sending packaged food, varied meals, and daily or bulk meal plans.

Potential services include prepared meal packages, varied food selections, daily plans, and larger family packages. Before launch, Asa must define the supplier relationship, food-safety responsibility, allergens, dietary preferences, portion sizes, delivery windows, substitutions, refunds, and whether Asa acts as an agent, marketplace, or principal seller.

### آسا پزشک — Asa Pezeshk

**Meaning:** coordinating and following up on family medical logistics.

Potential services include booking appointments, reminders, practical follow-up, accompanying a family member to a treatment center, coordinating suitable transport, and sending an authorized factual update. Asa must not diagnose, treat, interpret results, administer medication, make clinical decisions, or represent itself as a healthcare provider unless the model is separately licensed and approved.

## Recommended launch interpretation

The three names can support a broad brand vision from day one, but the operational launch should remain narrow:

1. Launch a defined subset of **آسا همراه** and **آسا پزشک** in one Tehran service zone after payment, legal, insurance, safeguarding, and staffing gates pass.
2. Validate **آسا خوراک** through an authorized supplier pilot before promising recurring or bulk meal delivery.
3. Use the three names consistently across the website, customer forms, marketing, service reports, and financial model.

## Suggested navigation labels

- آسا همراه — Practical companionship
- آسا خوراک — Packaged meals and food plans
- آسا پزشک — Medical-family coordination

The names describe the customer’s intended outcome; the service pages must still show exact inclusions, exclusions, prices, service area, consent rules, and cancellation/refund policies.
