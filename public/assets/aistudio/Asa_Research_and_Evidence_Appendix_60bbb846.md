## Claims and calculations

- **C-001:** The current payment model is not launch-ready. Supported by S-008–S-029 and provider-specific evidence; confidence high for provider non-approval, medium for ultimate legal outcome.
- **C-002:** Diaspora demand is plausible but not quantified. S-001 gives population context; no source measures willingness to pay for Asa.
- **C-003:** Tehran is the best conditional pilot city. This is a recommendation based on urban density, healthcare/retail access, and the Tehran-specific caregiver evidence in S-002/S-003; it is not a proven city ranking.
- **C-004:** Existing alternatives are strong for commodity delivery but weaker on accountable cross-border coordination and authorized family reporting. S-005–S-007 support the alternatives; the differentiation judgment is Asa’s hypothesis.
- **C-005:** The financial model is illustrative. All prices, wages, volumes, CAC, fees, and retention inputs are assumptions unless explicitly labeled otherwise.

## Open research questions

1. Which entity, ownership and management structure can obtain provider approval?
2. Which named acquirer and settlement bank will approve the disclosed Iran flow in writing?
3. What Iranian registrations, tax, labor, insurance, privacy, and service permissions apply in the selected Tehran zone?
4. What is the principal-versus-agent/payment-services classification in each customer market?
5. What will customers actually pay for Shopping and Medical Companion?
6. Can vetted workers deliver consistently within the target travel-time zone?
7. What is the repeat-purchase frequency after a successful first service?
8. What data must be collected for a family update, and what must never be collected?

## Evidence limitations

The research did not conduct interviews, surveys, legal opinions, provider onboarding, or a live payment test. Competitor pricing was not reliably visible in the sources reviewed. Public diaspora counts are not the addressable market. Iranian local-law research remains incomplete. These limitations are why the recommendation is conditional rather than a confident go decision.
