# Asa (آسا) Planning Package

Prepared 12 October 2026 from the founder brief and public web research. Updated 14 November 2026 with the official service naming architecture: **آسا همراه**, **آسا خوراک**, and **آسا پزشک**.

## Start here

1. `Asa_Executive_Summary.md` — payment gate, recommendation, economics, and decisions.
2. `Asa_Full_Business_Plan.md` — market, competitors, services, operations, risks, and strategy.
3. `Asa_Full_Marketing_Plan.md` — positioning, campaigns, channels, SEO, referrals, and metrics.
4. `Asa_Financial_Model.xlsx` — editable assumptions, unit economics, forecast, break-even, and sensitivity.
5. `Asa_Launch_Playbook.md` — 30-day validation and 90-day conditional launch plan.
6. `Asa_Website_and_Brand_Brief.md` — bilingual site structure, copy, FAQs, and minimum functionality.
7. `Asa_Research_and_Evidence_Appendix.md` — source register, payment evidence, claims, and open questions.
8. `Asa_Launch_Decision_Memo.md` — short partner/investor-facing decision note.
9. `Asa_Service_Naming_Update.md` — official service names, scope, and launch interpretation.

## Important limitation

This package is research and planning, not legal, tax, sanctions, medical, insurance, or investment advice. It does not establish provider approval, customer demand, or Iranian local-law compliance. The proposed international payment model is treated as a launch blocker until the exact entity, flow, counterparties, service scope, provider, and settlement bank are reviewed and approved in writing.
